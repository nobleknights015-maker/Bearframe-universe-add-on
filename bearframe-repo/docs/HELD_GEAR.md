# Held gear — 0.11.2 candidate

Inventory icons, item IDs, damage and loot remain unchanged. Each weapon has its own cuboid model, 64x64 original material texture, attachable and first/third-person pose. Geometry is bound to the equipped hand using the documented Bedrock attachable mechanism. Color and silhouette follow the supplied concept sheet, including a fixed curved chain for Hush Pendulum (not simulated physics). No global player geometry override or extra gameplay scripts. Static UV/link/PNG checks added. Exact in-game grip, scale, swing, visibility and shading require live client QA; no claim of emissive lighting or special attack animations. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/documents/attachables and https://wiki.bedrock.dev/items/attachables.
