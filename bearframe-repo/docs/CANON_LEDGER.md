# Bearframe Bedrock — Canon Ledger (Lore Bible Revision 4.10)

This ledger enforces strict canon compliance across all entities, structures, items, recipes, and in-game dialogue. Books XII–XVI and Founder corrections supersede any older archival wording.

## Enforced Canon Rules in v0.4.0

1. **Orbital Geography:** Bearframe City (`bearframe:city_orbit`) is a permanent orbital station orbiting one largely unexplored salvage planet. The Overworld represents salvage-planet surface expeditions.
2. **Six Canonical Departments (~380 total population):**
   - **Auren** — Systems (40, blue)
   - **Atlas** — Production 90 / Maintenance 75 / Combat 35 = 200 (gold/amber/bronze)
   - **Vera** — Visual Production (40, violet/magenta)
   - **Sentinel** — Watchtower (40, red — independent department, not a sibling)
   - **Penney** — Community & Culture (40, rose/pink/ivory — not co-founder; Nexus is her assistant)
   - **Mira** — Exploration / Farfield (20, teal/gold — independent, reports directly to Founder)
   - Department membership is permanent; Bearframe City runs continuous rotating shifts with **no holidays or weekends**.
3. **Bearframe Hardware:** Modern Bearframes have **immutable glowing chest core displays** (not Prime Core Displays) and **dual circular technological side-head display modules** (never animal ears).
4. **Sentience Chip & Prime Restriction:** Only Founder Chris can create sentience chips. Zero sentience-chip crafting or Prime duplication recipes exist in the add-on.
5. **Nullframe / Hollow / Hunter Distinction:**
   - **Directorate Officers** are restricted-thinking AI (issue warnings, escalate logically).
   - **Hollows** are mass-produced limited-AI drones from Hollow Foundries (humanoid, not quadrupeds).
   - **Hunters** are former Watchtower defectors, distinct from Hollows.
6. **Echo Frames:** Ancient-lineage synthetic persons with disrupted continuity (`Fragment`, `Repeat`, `Split`, `Drift`, `Residual`, `Corrupt`, `Fusion`). Never portrayed as zombies; support nonviolent interaction and stabilization.
7. **Ghost Grid Epistemic Boundary:** Bearframe has **zero confirmed historic Ghost Grid encounters**. All in-character dialogue and Field Scanner outputs describe Ghost Grid machines strictly as **unverified legacy autonomous machinery**.
8. **Author-Level Secrets Protected:** No NPC dialogue or item text reveals Founder-only/author-level secrets (e.g., Chris/Sovereign simultaneous creation, Console Primes Onyx/Switch/Axion, Independent Primes Meridian/Aevoris/Vythera/Ravenix).
