# Style guide

Use the Infinity palette as a compatibility target. Sampled stone panels are warm gray, bright industrial blocks are neutral, and older copper is subdued green. Use lighter whites around the surveyor silhouette, graphite at the joints, teal for Farfield and a clear chest display plus side-head modules. Age the walker with brown steel, cyan status light and growth attached to its housing. Keep the Hollow's shell regular and its bright red optic small and readable. Wreckage should mark a real route or prior use. Test the stock vanilla look as well as Infinity.

The generated mechanical models are editable Bedrock geometry with original PNG atlases and locomotion/idle animations. There are no finished graphical client screenshots.
