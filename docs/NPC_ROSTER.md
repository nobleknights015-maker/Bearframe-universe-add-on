# NPC roster

| Entity | Checkpoint role | Appearance and movement |
|---|---|---|
| Farfield Surveyor | Independent person, offers dialogue and a small charcoal-for-torches exchange | White/silver humanoid, dark joints, teal displays, field pack. Walk, idle and glance behavior. |
