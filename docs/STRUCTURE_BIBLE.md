# Maintenance relay

The site is a 21×17 footprint with a settling foundation, three entries, an open main hall and a separated service wing. Its original job was to condition a small service circuit. Its builders and cause of abandonment are unverified. Broken roofing, oxidized ductwork, loss of lighting and debris in the copper service line show where it failed. Farfield is investigating it during the optional gameplay encounter.

Approach from a paved entrance. The lectern, copper breaker, cobblestone service obstruction, iron-bar seal and recovery locker each have a purpose. The repair race is protected by site state. The surveyor offers light supplies. The walking maintenance unit can be examined. A perimeter Hollow is the only authored hostile here. There is a local schematic in the service room, ordinary block salvage remains available, and no quest item is needed to leave. A future iteration can add logs, a proper route from orbit and evidence for the builders.

The native plains placement is one candidate per randomized 22-chunk grid cell, subject to biome and engine checks. The buried entrance markers can grade a short, supported path within already loaded terrain if headroom and a dry connection are available. The grading pass uses air or water above suitable natural ground and skips obstructed paths.
