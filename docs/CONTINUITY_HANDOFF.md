# Continue Bearframe Bedrock

Start from the independent Git checkpoint and the 0.3.0 packages. The ten surface sites and orbital city are the current playtest boundary. Preserve the `bearframe:` IDs and relay phase values. Read CANON_LEDGER.md and the complete uploaded 4.10 lore master before adding lore-facing encounters. Leave Starbound Frontier paused. Obtain real-device footage and hands-on two-player QA before extending the world. Prioritize clearer combat tells, the surveyor's routine and the walker's repair behaviors. Add canon events only after Founder approval.

0.2.0 adds the survey region catalogs in `regions.js`, orbit and site services in `expedition.js`, and the per-player survey log in `survey_log.js`. Region indices and phase values are persistent in placed blocks. Append any new region definitions and template slots to preserve existing saves. The four orbital public rooms are playable; named canon characters and orbital economy would require another content pass.

0.3.0 adds four optional sites under the appended region indices in `ruins.js` and the authoring functions in `tools/ruin_layouts.mjs`. Signal Shelter is a deliberately wet route with dry breathing spaces. The dry Spillway variant has a one-time water check inside the authored tunnel and chambers.
